The House

One light,
one idea,
six silhouettes.

midi. was born of a simple conviction: fashion should be judged in the most honest hour of the day.

The Founder

Camille Ferrand

Trained in the Parisian ateliers, Camille Ferrand returned in 2019 to her native Gulf with one precise frustration: fashion is drawn under studio lighting, never under the light that actually matters. midi. was born of that observation — a house that cuts, fits and presents every piece at the hour when the sun leaves nothing hidden.

"I no longer wanted to dress imaginary silhouettes. I wanted to dress the light I know."

The Atelier

A place, not a factory.
